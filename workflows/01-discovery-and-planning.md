# Workflow 01 — Discovery and Planning

## Goal
Convert an idea into an approved, testable product definition.

## Inputs
`project/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, user conversation, available research sources.

## Steps
1. Inspect the project and identify existing assets.
2. Extract problem, users, context, constraints and desired outcomes.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses and open questions.
4. Research market/user/technical claims when tools and authorization permit; cite sources and dates.
5. Identify risks, alternatives and MVP scope.
6. Draft Product Vision, PRD, personas, journeys, business rules and roadmap.
7. Create measurable acceptance criteria and success metrics.
8. Present unresolved decisions and trade-offs.

## Approval gate
Do not begin material implementation until the user approves the product scope and key business rules.

## Outputs
Approved product docs, assumptions register, decision records and prioritized backlog.
