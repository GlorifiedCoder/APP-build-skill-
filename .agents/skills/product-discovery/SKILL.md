# Skill: Product Discovery

## When to use
Use when a new idea or product direction needs clarification.

## Required inputs
- Relevant project brief and approved specifications.
- Current repository state and related tests.
- Applicable architecture, security and design rules.

## Workflow
1. Read the project brief and conversation context.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns.
3. Define problem, users, alternatives and value proposition.
4. Identify validation research and evidence gaps.
5. Propose a narrow MVP and explicit non-goals.
6. Draft measurable outcomes and risks.
7. Ask the owner to approve scope-critical decisions.

## Expected outputs
Product brief, assumptions register, research plan, MVP recommendation.

## Quality rules
- Do not invent facts, test results or approvals.
- Cite external research where used.
- Identify assumptions and unresolved risks.
- Respect `AGENTS.md` approval requirements.
- Update project documentation when the outcome changes the source of truth.
